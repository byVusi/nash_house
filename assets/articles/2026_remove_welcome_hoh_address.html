Tiri Pasipanodya, Head of House


Good evening, Parents and Class of 2031, and welcome to Nash House.

My name is Tirivashe Pasipanodya and I am your Head of House for 2027. I would like to firstly say thank you for choosing St John’s College, however, you are actually lucky to be apart of this amazing House.

“A dream for many, a reality for few.”

Conando Vincimus, this is the Nash House Motto. I would like to emphasise this to you all. You are now apart of a legacy.

I know every Head of House will say that their House is a family but Nash House has truly become a piece of me. A piece of my soul. A place where I have met extended brothers whose shoulders I can cry on, which I did last week actually. I would like to create that for you guys.

Lastly, Parents. I would like to thank you for your trust. As their first Head of House, I am sincerely thanking you for placing your trust in my hands, as their new older brother, and the other older brothers. So thank you.

Welcome to Nash House 🩵 Conando Vincimus
